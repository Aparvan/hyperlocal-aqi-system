✨ 23% Error Reduction over IDW

Standard GIS Inverse Distance Weighting ignores local traffic corridors, boundary layer inversions, and satellite optical depth. On unseen spatial holdout stations, our Multimodal Deep Learning network reduces RMSE from 141.70 µg/m³ to 120.95 µg/m³ and MAE from 107.86 to 83.12 µg/m³ (Pearson r increases from 0.57 to 0.84).
